# V0.6 Sprint Backlog

## Goal

V0.6 evolves the system from "manual recognition rail with foundation taxonomy" (V0.5.x) to "live source-of-truth integration with structured leak detection and operational orchestration."

The sprint has three intertwined themes:

- Live sync from Anchor, FC, and QBO replaces static seeds.
- Verdict-driven leak detection gains proper agreement-state awareness.
- Operational orchestration adds pipeline runs, run logs, and dashboard surfaces.

## Probable Sub-Slice Decomposition

This is the working frame for V0.6 planning. Final decomposition will be decided during the V0.6 spec.

- V0.6.A — Data Foundation Layer: live sync extensions for Anchor agreement status, FC tags, QBO products, and canonical service alias resolution.
- V0.6.B — Verdict-Driven Leak Detection: 14-verdict taxonomy in DB, classification persistence, and leak surface UI.
- V0.6.C — Pipeline Orchestration: scheduled Recognition Pipeline, on-demand refresh button, and run log surface.
- V0.6.D — SLA Management Dashboard: per-staff workload, per-client SLA status, and breach detection.

### Verdict Taxonomy

- `INTERNAL_FAMILY` — Saez family, SBC; permanent suppress.
- `INACTIVE_FORMER_CLIENT` — Client churned. Permanent suppress. Conditions (all required): FC client inactive AND no active Anchor agreement AND zero QBO open balance AND no service-delivery FC tasks completed in last 365 days. Distinct from `INTERNAL_FAMILY` (suppressed for relationship reason) and `ENGAGEMENT_DECLINED` (period-scoped, may re-engage). If any one signal flips back on (new FC task, new Anchor agreement, new invoice), auto-reclassify out of this verdict.
- `PENDING_ENGAGEMENT_DRAFT` — Anchor agreement DRAFT, not sent.
- `PENDING_ENGAGEMENT_SENT` — Anchor agreement SENT, awaiting client.
- `INVOICE_OUTSTANDING_PAYMENT_PENDING` — Agreement and invoice exist; awaiting customer payment. Distinct from `PENDING_ENGAGEMENT_SENT` (no invoice yet) and `BILLING_SETUP_GAP` (recurring invoices not firing). Operational queue: AR collections; aging signal in 30/60/90 day buckets.
- `LEGACY_ENGAGEMENT_PRE_ANCHOR` — Legacy engagement valid (rolled forward from prior year); Anchor agreement intentionally not yet created. Per migration plan, Anchor agreement and invoice are created together at first invoicing time. Transitional verdict: should empty out as clients go through their first Anchor cycle. Auto-reclassify to `BILLING_OUTSIDE_AUDIT_WINDOW` or `consolidated_via_group_billed` when first Anchor invoice fires. Track "remaining pre-Anchor clients" as a migration health metric.
- `ENGAGEMENT_DECLINED` — Client declined this period (year-aware).
- `LEGITIMATE_LEAK` — No agreement, work being done.
- `BILLING_OUTSIDE_AUDIT_WINDOW` — Active agreement; annual cycle or first cycle not yet due.
- `BILLING_SETUP_GAP` — Active agreement for recurring service; recurring invoices not firing.
- `GROUP_DEFINITION_GAP` — Group's actually-billed parent missing from group definition.
- `MIXED` — Partial billing pattern, case-by-case.
- `CONSOLIDATED_VIA_GROUP_BILLED` — Work covered by an Anchor invoice billed on a group parent entity. Healthy / informational, not a leak. Default audit view hides these. See "Default leak surface should hide healthy rows" rule for examples.
- `SETTLED_VIA_QUICKBOOKS_PAYMENT` — Cash collected via QBO without an Anchor agreement/invoice trail. Distinct from `CONSOLIDATED_VIA_GROUP_BILLED` (no Anchor invoice anywhere) and `LEGACY_ENGAGEMENT_PRE_ANCHOR` (no planned Anchor migration). Temporary suppression: surfaced in the Anchor backfill queue (V0.6.D), not in the leak surface. Auto-transitions out when an Anchor agreement is created and first invoice fires. Real example observed 2026-05-06: Tropaholic group (`Rossbach, Christopher (1040)` + `Tropaholic LLC`) — paid via QBO directly, no Anchor agreement on file.

Likely slice: V0.6.B.

### Verdict Logic Requirements

- Audit reads Anchor agreement status and last invoice date for each FC client, not just invoice presence.
- Service-type-aware audit window: 180 days for monthly recurring, 365 days for annual tax-only.
- Year-aware classification for `ENGAGEMENT_DECLINED` (`declined_for_TY:2025` vs. permanent).
- Re-emergence detection: classified row with new FC tasks is flagged for reclassification.
- Aging signal: days since state change (`SENT > 60` = follow-up; `> 90` = stale).
- Verdict persistence: once classified, suppress from default audit view unless state changes.
- Bulk classification UI: checkboxes plus dropdown for fast triage. → V0.6.B
- Scheduled state change tracking: classifications support a `re_evaluate_at` date column. Use case: client transitioning billing models on a known date (e.g., Celtic Auto post-pay → subscription June 2026). Daily job lists due-for-reclassification entries; UI flags them for review. → V0.6.B
- Auto-transition verdicts on state change: when an Anchor invoice fires for a `LEGACY_ENGAGEMENT_PRE_ANCHOR` client, auto-reclassify to `BILLING_OUTSIDE_AUDIT_WINDOW` or `consolidated_via_group_billed` per service type. Similar auto-transitions: `PENDING_ENGAGEMENT_SENT` → `consolidated_via_group_billed` when agreement signs and invoice fires; `INVOICE_OUTSTANDING_PAYMENT_PENDING` → satisfied when cash collected. Goal: minimize manual reclassification. → V0.6.B
- Migration health metrics: track count and aging of `LEGACY_ENGAGEMENT_PRE_ANCHOR` rows as a top-line metric. Trend should decrease as clients complete first Anchor cycle. Useful for migration progress reporting. → V0.6.B
- Default leak surface should hide healthy rows: `consolidated_via_group_billed` rows are not leaks; they're informational confirmations. Default audit view should exclude them. Provide a "Show all (including healthy)" toggle for full visibility. Real examples: (1) observed 2026-05-04 — Hornauer 1040 + NDH 1065 appearing in audit output despite being correctly billed via DVH consolidated invoice; (2) observed 2026-05-06 — Wong family 1040s (Kam F & Choi L Wong; Wong, Ken & Nancy) appearing despite being correctly billed via KNW Properties and Assets LLC consolidated invoice. → V0.6.B
- Distinguish service-delivery FC tasks from administrative tasks in audit: current audit script flags any FC client with completed tasks in 180 days, regardless of task type. Clients with only onboarding/admin tasks completed generate false-positive leak signals. V0.6 audit should classify FC tasks by type (service delivery vs. admin/onboarding) using project context, task title patterns, or an explicit task-type tag. → V0.6.A or V0.6.B
- Anchor service coverage vs. FC project coverage detection: Anchor agreements often cover multiple services (e.g., tax + year-end bookkeeping). FC may have projects for some of those services but not others, creating an operational gap where contracted services exist but aren't yet operationalized in FC. V0.6 should compare Anchor agreement service line items against FC project coverage for each client, surfacing services contracted but not yet project-ized. Useful both as a leak-detection refinement (audit flag may be due to this gap) and as a process hygiene signal (FC operational setup catching up to Anchor commitments). Real example observed 2026-05-04: Lee's Inc, Surber Roy (1040), Wolfsonstein LLC — Anchor agreements cover tax + year-end closing bookkeeping; FC has tax projects but year-end bookkeeping projects not yet created. Verdict still `BILLING_OUTSIDE_AUDIT_WINDOW` because billing not yet due, but the FC coverage gap is its own operational signal. → V0.6.A or V0.6.B
- Annual deliverable mid-cycle must not be hidden by monthly billing on a parent entity. When an Anchor agreement covers an annual return (1040, 1120, etc.) for an individual whose related business entity carries monthly recurring billing, audit must keep the annual deliverable classified as `BILLING_OUTSIDE_AUDIT_WINDOW` until the return is filed and the annual billing event fires. Do not auto-classify as `consolidated_via_group_billed` based on the parent's monthly invoices alone — those cover bookkeeping, not the 1040. Extension filings extend the in-progress window past April 15 and must not flip the verdict to `LEGITIMATE_LEAK` either. Real example observed 2026-05-06: Inatsuka, Linda (1040) — LTI Associates Inc has 6 monthly invoices totaling $648.96 in 180d for bookkeeping; Linda's 1040 is a separate annual deliverable, extension filed, return not yet completed. Correct verdict: `BILLING_OUTSIDE_AUDIT_WINDOW`. → V0.6.B
- Audit input filter: zero-signal rows must not surface. The audit query should pre-filter out clients where every signal is null/zero — no active FC engagement, no Anchor agreement, no invoices in window, not in staff assignments. These rows are stale QBO history of churned clients and only generate noise. Apply an `any-active-signal` filter at query time so churned clients don't enter the manual classification queue. Newly-churned clients should be classified once as `INACTIVE_FORMER_CLIENT` and persisted, then auto-suppressed. Real example observed 2026-05-06: Joy Property Management LLC — FC inactive, no Anchor, $0 QBO balance, not in assignments file; surfaced in audit only because of legacy QBO records. → V0.6.B
